# StudyOS

Collaborative AI Study Platform and Virtual Study Room with synchronized video lectures, in-app PDF reading (solo & group presentations), real-time live situation tracking with stopwatch & subject breakdown, Telegram cloud vault, and personal AI study coach.

## Features
- **📺 Synchronized Co-Study Video Theater**: Live synced playback, play/pause and timestamp synchronization.
- **📖 In-App PDF Reader**: Read exam study materials solo or present with live page turns synced to the group.
- **⏱️ Live Situation & Subject Tracker**: Real-time study stopwatches, daily hours tracker, and subject breakdown.
- **📑 Telegram Cloud Vault**: Store notes and PDFs with zero file-size limits via Telegram Bot API.
- **🎨 Interactive Whiteboard**: Collaborative drawing canvas for reasoning puzzles and math equations.
- **📝 Collaborative Notes**: Markdown and speed math formula notes.
- **🤖 Personal AI Coach**: Instant doubt solving, schedule planning, and exam shortcuts.
- **🔐 Group Rooms & Permanent Accounts**: Unique Room IDs and voice chat password protection.
